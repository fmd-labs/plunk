Topics split your marketing mail into lists a contact subscribes to separately: product news, a newsletter, event invitations. A project with topics sends every campaign for exactly one topic, to the contacts subscribed to it, and a contact who unsubscribes from one topic keeps receiving the others: the unsubscribe link of a campaign's email drops that campaign's topic only (see [Unsubscribe & preferences pages](/guides/unsubscribe-pages#topics)).

Turn topics on in **Settings → Topics** ("Send campaigns per topic"), then add the project's topics there or through the API.

## A topic

| Field                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `slug`               | Identifies the topic in the API: up to 64 lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`. It cannot change once the topic exists.                                                                              |
| `name`               | Shown in the dashboard.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `description`        | Optional.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `basis`              | `OPT_OUT` (the default): every contact is subscribed until it unsubscribes. `OPT_IN`: only contacts who subscribed are.                                                                                |
| `campaignSelectable` | Whether campaigns can be sent for the topic (default `true`). A topic kept for segment filters only sets it to `false`.                                                                                |
| `listId`             | Optional `List-Id` header for the topic's campaign emails, such as `Product news <product-news.example.com>` (RFC 2919, printable ASCII). Mailbox providers use it to tell the topic's mail apart.     |

## A contact's subscription

A contact's subscription to a topic is its explicit state for that topic, or without one the topic's basis. Set explicit states:

- on the contact's page in the dashboard (the **Topics** card);
- with `PUT /contacts/:id/topics`, for example `{"topics": {"product-news": false}}`. `null` removes the explicit state, so the basis decides again;
- with `POST /contacts/batch`, through each contact's `topics` field.

`GET /contacts/:id/topics` lists every topic with the contact's state (`subscribed`, and `explicit`, which is `null` when the basis decides).

The contact's own **Subscribed** state keeps its meaning of "may receive marketing at all": bounces and complaints still turn it off. A campaign reaches a contact only when both hold.

## What changes in a project with topics

- **Campaigns** need a topic before they are sent, and reach only the contacts of their audience who are subscribed to it. A campaign without a valid topic reaches nobody.
- **Transactional campaigns** are not available; send mail that must ignore subscriptions through [`/v1/send`](/concepts/transactional-emails).
- **Workflows and `/v1/send`** send transactional templates only; a marketing or headless template is refused, so marketing mail always goes through a campaign and its topic. A `/v1/send` without a template is your own transactional mail.

Turning topics off again keeps every campaign that has a topic limited to it, so a scheduled campaign never reaches more contacts than it was planned for; such a campaign keeps its topic but cannot take a new one. A scheduled campaign that has no usable topic when it is due goes back to a draft instead of sending.

## Contacts created by transactional sends

Upstream Plunk creates the contact of a transactional send unsubscribed, so "subscribed" doubles as marketing consent. When topics hold the consent, turn on **Subscribe contacts created by transactional sends** (Settings → Topics): such contacts then start subscribed, and "subscribed" only means "not suppressed" (bounces and complaints turn it off).

With it on, a contact a transactional send creates is in the audience of every opt-out topic it has not left, so set its topic states when you create it, or keep consent-based topics opt-in. Contacts created unsubscribed before stay out of every campaign, whatever their topic states say, until they are subscribed again.

## Segments

Every topic is a yes/no field in the segment builder (category **Topics**). Through the API, filter on `topic.<topicId>` with `equals` or `notEquals` and `true` or `false`:

```json
{"field": "topic.4f9c…", "operator": "equals", "value": true}
```

## API

| Method | Path                   | What it does                                                   |
| ------ | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GET    | `/topics`              | List the project's topics.                                     |
| PUT    | `/topics/:slug`        | Create the topic with this slug (`201`), or update it (`200`). |
| DELETE | `/topics/:slug`        | Delete a topic and every contact's state for it.               |

An update changes only the fields it gives: leaving `basis` out keeps it. Changing an opt-in topic to opt-out subscribes every contact without an explicit state.
| GET    | `/contacts/:id/topics` | A contact's subscription to each topic.                        |
| PUT    | `/contacts/:id/topics` | Set a contact's explicit state for some topics, by slug.       |

Deleting a topic keeps the stats of campaigns sent for it; drafts lose their topic and need a new one. It is refused (`409`) while a campaign is scheduled or sending for the topic, or a segment or such a campaign filters on it.
